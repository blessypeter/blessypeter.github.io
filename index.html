01 / About

About me

The first thing I noticed in Grady's CVICU wasn't the equipment. It was a Code Blue, and a room full of people moving toward one patient like a single system.

Everyone knew exactly where to be. Nobody had to ask. I came to Grady's TELP program hoping to learn the language of healthcare, and I left understanding the people behind it. The drama isn't the machines. It's the coordination. And somehow, in the middle of all that, they still made time to answer my questions.

I'm a senior on the Health Science pathway at FCS Innovation Academy, working toward pre-med and neuroscience. The questions I keep coming back to sit where genetics meets the brain: how a string of DNA ends up shaping how a mind works, and what happens when something in that code goes wrong.

When I'm not at the hospital or in the lab, I'm usually around people. I help underclassmen find their way through the Health Science pathway, I was Social Chair for 21st Century Leaders, and every summer I lead a small group of kids at my church's Vacation Bible School. I grew up speaking English and Tamil, and I've added Hindi and Spanish along the way.
